About CostPilot AI

Authoritative estimation pipeline and methodology

CostPilot AI is an enterprise software cost estimation platform designed to replace unverified arbitrary price guesses with defensible, mathematically grounded engineering estimates.

1. Empirical Bayes Priors

Every discrete feature is estimated using Three-Point PERT (Optimistic, Most Likely, Pessimistic) shrunk toward historical category distributions from 44,301 features.

2. Supervised Random Forest

Trained scikit-learn models predict top-down effort, cost, and duration benchmarks with zero target leakage and held-out cross-validation.

3. Financial Invariants

Strict financial consistency: Labour Cost = ∑(Allocated Role Hours × Hourly Rate). Contingency is applied strictly once to pre-contingency totals.

Brooks' Law Coordination & Critical Path

Software development capacity is not strictly linear. As team size increases, communication coordination channels scale at n(n-1)/2, reducing individual productive throughput. Feature dependencies are modeled as a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) to calculate the real critical path.
